Copycat Cracker Barrel Meatloaf

Home

Description

This copycat Cracker Barrel meatloaf recipe delivers the same delicious flavor you'd get at the restaurant, but with a simpler preparation process. The key is in the blend of ketchup, mustard, and Worcestershire sauce that creates that signature tangy sweetness.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 egg, beaten

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, breadcrumbs, milk, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and beaten egg. Mix until well combined.
  3. Shape the mixture into a loaf and place it in a greased baking dish.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven for 1 hour or until the meatloaf is cooked through and reaches an internal temperature of 160°F (70°C).
  5. Let the meatloaf rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
