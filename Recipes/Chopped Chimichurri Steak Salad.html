Chopped Chimichurri Steak Salad

Home

Description

This chopped chimichurri steak salad is fresh, vibrant, and packed with bold flavors from juicy seared steak, crisp vegetables, and bright homemade chimichurri. Perfect for lunch or dinner, it’s a hearty yet refreshing salad that's put together in 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak, trimmed
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese

Instructions

  1. Season the steak with salt and pepper. Grill or pan-sear until desired doneness is reached, about 5-7 minutes per side.
  2. Let the steak rest for a few minutes before slicing it thinly against the grain.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and feta cheese.
  4. Add the sliced steak to the salad and toss to combine.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
