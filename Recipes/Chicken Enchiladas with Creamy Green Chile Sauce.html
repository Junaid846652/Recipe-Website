Chicken Enchiladas with Creamy Green CHile Sauce

Home

Description

This recipe makes chicken- and cheese-filled corn tortillas baked under a creamy sauce infused with hot peppers. Adjust the amount of chopped chiles to suit your taste!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Monterey Jack)
  • 1/2 cup chopped onions
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 8-10 corn tortillas
  • 2 cups creamy green chile sauce (recipe below)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, cheese, onions, cilantro, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Warm the corn tortillas in a skillet or microwave until pliable.
  4. Spoon about 1/4 cup of the chicken mixture onto each tortilla and roll it up. Place the rolled tortillas seam-side down in a baking dish.
  5. Pour the creamy green chile sauce over the enchiladas, making sure they are well covered.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the sauce is bubbly and the cheese is melted.
  7. Garnish with additional chopped cilantro if desired and serve hot.
